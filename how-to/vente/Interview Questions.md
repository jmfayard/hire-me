


## The interviewer

- What is your role and scope at this company ?
- Why are you hiring for this position?
- Why are you hiring now ?
- What does success looks like from the new hire ?
- Why did **you** choose to work here?
- Who are your heroes in the company?
- What is your target salary range?


## Culture

What surprise most the new people when you start working at XXX ?  
  
What is your favorite part of working there ?  
  
What are the three adjectives you would pick to describe the work culture ? And why ?  
  
What is the most important tip you would give to someone applying there ?

What is the busiest time of the year / when do I need do to plan for overtime?

Do you have on call / monitoring / alerting in place? How long do you stay on call?

How many people have been promoted recently and what got them promoted?

What would you say is the kind of people that thrive at XXX and why ?

What kind of people left prematurely and why?

What is your favorite way to communicate inside the company ?


## Company

- How does your company make money?
	*Is your company currently profitable?*
- What's the biggest change your group has gone through in the last year?
- Which competitors are you most worried about?
- How diverse is the company personal? 
	*Nationalities / Gender / Languages / Non tech*


## The team

- Who would I I be working within the organization?

- How do you communicate inside the team and with other groups inside the company?

- Do you have any Open Source-related policies? 
	*Could be anything from allowing individuals to do it / actively supporting them / paying open-source maintainers of projects we rely on / maintaining own open-source projects?*


## Coding practices

- What kind of craft practices do you have in place ? 
(CI, CD, pair programming, architecture, testing strategy, ....)

- Do you have a one step build process and a continuous integration process implemented?

- What is your preferred approach to test things correctly?
  Test pyramid unit vs integration test / Methodology / testing methodology / current level of test coverage / preferred libraries

- What is your preferred approach to document things? 
	*README.md vs tutorials vs PR descriptions vs self-documented tests vs Wiki vs blog posts.*


## If working remotely

- What timezone/hours does the company work in?
- Do your developers pair program on a regular basis?
- What are your primary and secondary channels for communications?
- What are your main struggles related to working remotely?
